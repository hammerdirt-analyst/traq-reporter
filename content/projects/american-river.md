Elmanto River Access, usually written El Manto Access, is a public entry point to the American River Parkway in Rancho Cordova.  It is a neighborhood-friendly way to get down to the river for walking, biking, fishing, swimming, kayaking, relaxing, and beach/river access. 

El Manto River Access is one of Rancho Cordova’s main neighborhood access points to the American River   —good for trail access, river lounging, and casual outdoor use — but it is best approached with normal river caution, especially around deeper water and banks.

It is also a great place to study oak trees and other species common along the parkway
