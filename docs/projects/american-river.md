<div class="text-block" markdown="1">
El Manto Access, is a public entry point to the American River Parkway in Rancho Cordova.  It is a neighborhood-friendly way to get down to the river for walking, biking, fishing, swimming, kayaking, relaxing, and beach/river access. 

It is also a great place to study oak trees and other species common along the parkway
