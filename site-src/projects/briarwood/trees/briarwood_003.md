<div class="text-block" markdown="1">
The target assessment identifies the patio as the primary target area, which is occupied by two individuals. The tree is situated within 1x its height from the patio, placing it at a distance of approximately 42 feet. This proximity to the target area necessitates a careful evaluation of the tree's health and structural integrity.

The tree exhibits a high vigor with 100% of its foliage appearing normal and no signs of abiotic stress or pest infestations. The species profile indicates that V-shaped unions and crossing branches can potentially weaken branches and leaders, but the overall condition of the tree remains stable. The likelihood of failure impacting the patio is categorized as low, given the distance and the current health of the tree.

The crown of the tree is dense with a large relative crown size, and while there are over-extended branches, the likelihood of failure is deemed improbable. The trunk has been noted to have vertical cracks, but these are classified as minor defects with an improbable likelihood of failure. The tree has been professionally maintained, which contributes to its overall structural integrity.

The roots and root collar show no signs of decay, damage, or significant concerns. The root plate is stable, and there are no visible issues with the collar depth or soil weakness. The tree's load factors are stable, with no recent changes observed that would affect its structural stability.

The overall risk associated with the tree is classified as low, with mitigation options including ongoing maintenance to ensure continued health and stability. After implementing these maintenance practices, the expected residual risk remains low, indicating that the tree is unlikely to pose a threat to the surrounding targets.
